# L3 Questions (7–11 yrs)

<div class="badges" markdown><span class="badge badge-l3">L3 · Senior/Lead</span></div>

At this level interviewers check that you can choose between patterns and defend the choice to a team. Expect "when would you *not* use X", "what breaks first", and "how would you know", across sagas, outboxes, CQRS, consistency, resilience, tracing, SLOs, deployment and performance.

**How to answer.** State the decision, the alternative you rejected, and the evidence. The chapters give you evidence in numbers: "retries at three layers sent 27 calls per request to a dead dependency; a 10% retry budget, 1.4" is the kind of sentence that separates a senior answer from a textbook one. Close with how you'd verify it in production.

**Where to spend time.** Two thirds of all questions are L3. The architecture-patterns part carries the most (111). The [L3 learning path](../getting-started/index.md#learning-paths) and the decision guides in each part's overview are the fastest revision.
