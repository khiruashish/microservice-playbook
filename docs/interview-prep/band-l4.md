# L4 Questions (11–15+ yrs)

<div class="badges" markdown><span class="badge badge-l4">L4 · Architect</span></div>

At this level interviewers check that you can shape a system and an organisation over years. Expect questions on decomposing a monolith and in what order, team boundaries, consistency across a whole platform, multi-region and tenancy, platform engineering, governance, cost, and when not to use microservices at all.

**How to answer.** Frame the decision by its forces (team size, change rate, risk, cost), give two or three options with what each costs, choose, and say how you'd know you were wrong and what you'd do then. Name the organisational side: who owns it, how it's reviewed, how it's kept. The [architect & leadership rounds](architect-leadership-rounds.md) page practises that structure.

**Where to spend time.** Part 2's migration and anti-pattern chapters, Part 4's consistency chapters and Part 11 carry the most. Each chapter's section 8 (the ADR and the industry analogues) is written for this band.
